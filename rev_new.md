# 2026 Year-End Written Feedback

## 1. Strengths and Achievements

2026 was a year of significant delivery, technical leadership, and platform transformation. I played a key role in evolving the Calculation Framework Runtime into a scalable, observable, and resilient enterprise platform supporting critical financial reporting. My responsibilities extended beyond technical execution to include architecture, platform strategy, delivery leadership, stakeholder alignment, and operational accountability.

### Strategic Delivery & Technical Leadership

I led key architectural and delivery decisions for the strategic **Observability capability**, establishing centralized calculator execution, SLA, performance, and historical reporting services. The solution progressed into production, creating a reusable foundation for operational transparency, execution traceability, and improved production support.

I helped expand **multi-tenant Airflow orchestration**, enabling NSFR and IPV onboarding and adoption by two additional business teams. The reusable architecture strengthened tenant isolation, scalability, and infrastructure efficiency while establishing a sustainable onboarding model for future calculator workloads.

I also advanced **event-driven NSFR execution**, eliminating a manual trigger step, and led the technical design of governed **On-Demand Calculator execution**. These initiatives support more efficient and controlled calculation workflows, with a proposed opportunity to move eligible execution earlier in the financial close cycle from Working Day 5 to Working Day 2.

Additionally, I contributed to foundational execution metadata, API contracts, and tenant-level attribution patterns supporting **per-run cost analytics**, improving cost visibility and enabling future optimization capabilities.

### Operational Resilience & Risk Management

I strengthened platform security, reliability, and operational readiness, contributing to **100% remediation of identified critical VM15 vulnerabilities**, component modernization, and BCM recovery validation.

I established evidence-based readiness expectations for platform upgrades, emphasizing compatibility testing, production-representative validation, recovery rehearsals, and controlled rollback strategies. Broader Runtime improvements, including Control DAG enhancements, were associated with a reported reduction in operational incidents from **more than 180 to zero**, reflecting our continued focus on platform stability and risk reduction.

### Engineering Excellence & Innovation

I helped drive substantial improvements in Runtime Pod delivery performance, achieving a **212.5% increase in throughput and a 51.2% reduction in system lead time**. These results received Agile-coach recognition and the Space Rocket award.

I championed practical adoption of **GitHub Copilot**, with our H1 assessment associating AI-assisted engineering with approximately **20% higher sprint velocity**, faster implementation, automated testing, and improved test coverage. I also established a recurring **AI Power Hour** to promote knowledge sharing and sustainable AI adoption across the team.

Overall, I believe my contributions demonstrate exceptional performance through a combination of strategic platform delivery, technical leadership, measurable engineering improvements, and a sustained commitment to operational resilience and risk reduction.
